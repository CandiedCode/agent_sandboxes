# Presentation Requirements

**Note for Q section:** Instead of presenting in class, you will upload an 8-10 minute recording of your presentation by the deadline indicated in Canvas.

## Content needed for the presentation (with suggested time allocations)

1. **Project Title and Group Members**

2. **Introduction + Motivation/Background/Prior Work (~1 min):** Introduce very briefly what your project is about (i.e., the project scope and goals), and motivate its importance. If discussing background or prior work helps set the context for your project, then do so briefly.

3. **Approach (~3 mins):** Describe what your approach to the project has been. It'd be great to include any insights into challenges that you encountered and how you overcame them. For example:
   1. If you are developing an attack, discuss the design of your attack strategy.
   2. If you are developing a defense, discuss the design of your defense.
   3. If you are doing an empirical study (e.g., measuring or evaluating real-world security), discuss your measurement/evaluation methodology (e.g., data collection or analysis process).

4. **Results (Findings/Analysis/Evaluation) (~3 mins):** Describe the major outcomes or results of your project. If you developed an attack or a defense, you should discuss a careful evaluation of its effectiveness (in terms of security/privacy, and potentially along other dimensions such as performance). If you did an empirical study, discuss your analysis and findings.

5. **Discussion/Conclusion (~2 min):** Describe briefly what might be the security/privacy implications of your results, as well as potential future work that could expand on your project.

## Time budget

| Section | Time |
|---|---|
| 1. Title and group members | (not timed) |
| 2. Introduction + motivation/background/prior work | ~1 min |
| 3. Approach | ~3 min |
| 4. Results | ~3 min |
| 5. Discussion/conclusion | ~2 min |
| **Total** | **~9 min** (target 8-10) |
